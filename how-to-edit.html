Editing the Orientation deck

You change the slides right on the website. No downloads, no code. Phone, tablet, or computer. The weekly schedule is easiest on a computer.

Edit link (bookmark this one)dwightrfm.github.io/rise-orientation/?edit Presenting link (send this to the team)dwightrfm.github.io/rise-orientation

1Open the edit link

A bar shows at the top that says Editing. Use the ‹ › buttons to move between slides, or All slides to see every slide at once.

2Make your changes

  • Words: click any words and type. Enter starts a new line.
  • Pictures: they have a gold dashed border. Click one and upload a new picture.
  • QR codes: click the QR code, paste the new link, press Make QR code. Scan it with your phone to test it.
  • Weekly Zoom schedule (slide 11): point at a session to change its day, start time (Pacific), or color. + Add session adds one. The red ✕ removes one.
  • Big event (slide 12): click Big events in the top bar. Change the name, city, and dates. The countdown follows on its own.
  • Check your work: Preview shows the slides the way the team sees them.

3Use your own version (no key needed)

Your edits save on your device by themselves. On a phone or tablet, tap any words and a box opens for that slide. Present shows your version full size. My copy downloads it as one file that plays offline, or starts you over.

4Publish to team (key holders only)

The Publish to team button in the top corner saves everything to the live site. The site updates in 1 to 2 minutes.

The first time only, it asks for an edit key. Paste the team edit key. Your device remembers it after that.

Made a mistake? While typing, Ctrl+Z (Cmd+Z on a Mac) undoes it. After you publish, nothing is lost: every version is saved, and Dwight can bring back any earlier one.

Leaving with unsaved changes? The page warns you first.

Presenting: open the presenting link, press F for full screen, and use the arrow keys. On a phone or tablet: tap the right side to go forward, the left side to go back, and turn the phone sideways.
